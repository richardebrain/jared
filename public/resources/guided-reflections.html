CORE Values: Guided Reflections

Use these reflection questions to deepen your understanding of our CORE values and how to implement them in your classroom. You can print this page to write your answers by hand, or use it as a digital worksheet.

CORE Values: Guided Reflections

Consistency

1. What routines in your classroom help create consistency for your students?
2. Think about Miss Rosa's story. How did her consistent approach help her student feel secure?
3. What is one area where you could improve consistency in your teaching practice?

Openness

1. How do you demonstrate openness to new ideas in your classroom?
2. Consider Ms. Elena's story. How did her openness help build trust with the family?
3. What barriers might prevent you from being fully open with families or colleagues?

Respect

1. How do you model respectful behavior for your students?
2. What specific strategies do you use to show respect for diverse family backgrounds?
3. How might you better incorporate respect into challenging situations with students?

Excellence

1. What does excellence in early childhood education mean to you personally?
2. Think about Craig's story. How did the commitment to excellence transform his experience?
3. What is one professional goal you have that would help you achieve greater excellence?

Overall Reflection

Which CORE value do you feel most confident in demonstrating? Which one might need more development?
How will you incorporate what you've learned about CORE values into your daily practice?
