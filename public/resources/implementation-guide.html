CORE Values Implementation Guide

This guide outlines practical strategies for incorporating our CORE values into your daily classroom practice. For each value, we provide concrete actions, implementation tips, and examples of success.

Consistency

Daily Actions:

  • Establish and follow predictable classroom routines
  • Respond to similar situations in similar ways
  • Use consistent language when explaining rules and expectations
  • Follow through on promises and stated consequences

Implementation Tips:

  • Create visual schedules that children can reference throughout the day
  • Use transition cues (songs, chimes) consistently
  • Document your classroom procedures so substitutes can maintain consistency
  • Meet regularly with co-teachers to align your approaches

Success Example:

Miss Rosa created a morning greeting ritual where she welcomed each child by name and with the same greeting phrase. Children began to anticipate and participate in the greeting, and new or shy children quickly learned the routine, helping them feel secure even when experiencing separation anxiety.

Openness

Daily Actions:

  • Listen attentively to children's ideas and questions
  • Incorporate children's interests into curriculum
  • Welcome family input and participation
  • Be receptive to new teaching approaches and methods

Implementation Tips:

  • Keep an "ideas jar" where children can contribute topics they want to explore
  • Create multiple channels for family communication (app, email, notes)
  • Schedule regular time for professional reading or webinars
  • Practice active listening techniques with colleagues and children

Success Example:

Ms. Elena noticed a child's fascination with construction vehicles outside the classroom window. Instead of redirecting him back to the planned activity, she gathered books about construction and created a building center with toy vehicles. This led to a two-week exploration of buildings and construction that engaged the entire class and incorporated math, science, and literacy.

Respect

Daily Actions:

  • Use positive, affirming language with all children
  • Acknowledge and celebrate diverse backgrounds and abilities
  • Respect children's bodily autonomy and personal boundaries
  • Model respectful conflict resolution

Implementation Tips:

  • Get down to children's eye level when speaking with them
  • Include diverse materials and books in your classroom
  • Learn key phrases in children's home languages
  • Create a peace corner for respectful conflict resolution

Success Example:

A teacher noticed that during dramatic play, children were creating "families" that only reflected traditional structures. She added books and photos showing diverse family compositions and gently expanded children's play by asking open questions like "Who else might live in this home?" This led to more inclusive play scenarios where children felt their own family situations were respected and represented.

Excellence

Daily Actions:

  • Prepare thoughtful, developmentally appropriate activities
  • Reflect on your practice and seek continuous improvement
  • Keep detailed documentation of children's progress
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and inviting classroom environment

Implementation Tips:

  • Set aside weekly planning time with intentional goals
  • Keep a teacher journal to reflect on what's working and what isn't
  • Take photos of children's work to document learning journeys
  • Involve children in classroom organization and care

Success Example:

Craig's story exemplifies excellence in action. By creating a structured environment with clear expectations while still honoring his emotional needs, his teachers helped transform a challenging situation into a growth opportunity. They documented his progress meticulously, celebrated small wins, and continuously refined their approach based on what worked best for him.

Weekly Implementation Checklist

Use this checklist to ensure you're incorporating all CORE values in your practice:

  • I have maintained consistent routines and responses this week
  • I have demonstrated openness to children's ideas and family input
  • I have shown respect for all children's backgrounds and abilities
  • I have pursued excellence through preparation and reflection
  • I have documented examples of CORE values in action
  • I have shared CORE value successes with families
  • I have collaborated with colleagues to strengthen CORE implementation

CORE Values in Challenging Situations

Challenging Situation CORE Values Response
Child having difficulty separating from parent Consistency: Follow same greeting routine daily
Openness: Accept child's feelings without judgment
Respect: Honor child's emotions while providing support
Excellence: Prepare engaging activities for transition time
Conflict between children Consistency: Use same conflict resolution steps each time
Openness: Listen to both perspectives
Respect: Help children express feelings respectfully
Excellence: Document recurring conflicts to identify patterns
Family expressing concern about curriculum Consistency: Respond promptly as you would to any family
Openness: Consider their perspective without defensiveness
Respect: Acknowledge their role as their child's first teacher
Excellence: Prepare a thoughtful response with research-based support

Remember: CORE values work together as an integrated approach. When you demonstrate one value well, it often strengthens your ability to demonstrate the others.
