At the top:

“Good morning, Leader. What challenge can we help you lead through today?”

They’re given three options:

Option A: Handle a Situation (Situational Coaching)
Tapping this brings up a Situation Library, organized into real-world childcare management topics:

“A teacher is consistently late”

“I’m feeling burned out”

“There’s staff drama and tension”

“Parent is upset about a teacher”

“Low morale among team”

“My teacher is losing motivation”

“I need help giving feedback”

“How do I retain my best staff?”

They tap one → AI-powered Leadership Coach Module activates.

They receive:

A quick 2–3 sentence diagnosis of the situation

A short motivational insight (like a quote or punchy “you got this” phrase)

A 1-minute audio or animated “Mini Message” in a voice inspired by Tony, Brené, or Simon

3 coaching steps:

What to say (sample script)

What to observe in yourself and your team

One micro-action to take today

Finally:
“What’s your goal for this situation?”
The user types it in → system stores it, and offers to send a reminder in 2–7 days.

🔥 Option B: Boost Me (The Motivation Engine)
Clicking Boost Me brings up:

A 30–60 second voice/audio clip that says something like:

“You’ve got a room full of tiny humans counting on you—but they’re not the only ones learning. You’re doing sacred work. Leadership isn’t about perfection—it’s about showing up, listening hard, and making people feel safe to grow.”

It also offers:

A short breathing or re-centering exercise

A “Mantra of the Day” (e.g. “Calm is contagious”, “Presence over panic”)

This feature is used when the director just needs to reset their energy and shift their state.

🧰 Option C: Tools & Training
This is the on-demand library of printable and video-based leadership tools.

Includes:

One-pagers with leadership strategies (e.g. “How to run an inspiring 5-minute staff huddle”)

Downloadable scripts (for difficult conversations)

Micro-trainings (2–3 minute videos or audio explaining a skill: “How to coach without micromanaging”)

Templates for:

Giving feedback

Conflict mediation forms

Teacher observation checklists

Goal-setting trackers for staff

Everything is visual, clean, short, and practical.

📈 Micro Goals + Progress Tracker
After each coaching session, directors are invited to set a simple leadership intention or “goal”:

“I will have a check-in with my late teacher before Friday.”

“I’ll send a positive message to my staff tomorrow morning.”

These are stored and optionally scheduled for nudges and encouragement.

Weekly dashboard shows:

Wins achieved

Coaching completed

A “Streak” of leadership actions taken

🧠 Director Reflection Journal (Optional)
Weekly AI-guided journaling prompts:

“What leadership moment felt meaningful this week?”

“Where did I feel stretched?”

“What do I want to carry forward next week?”

All entries are private and used to help the AI coach understand your tone and growth needs.
