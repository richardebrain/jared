1. Chrome Music Lab – Song Maker
Chrome Music Lab’s Song Maker supports an embed endpoint so you can drop the full interactive sequencer right into your site:

html
Copy
Edit
<iframe
  src="https://musiclab.chromeexperiments.com/Song-Maker/embed/6498684503719936"
  width="600"
  height="400"
  frameborder="0"
  allowfullscreen>
</iframe>
– Replace the long number at the end with your Song-Maker share ID.
– Teachers can then draw melodies and rhythms live, no navigation away required.

2. BandLab Projects & Players
BandLab provides an iframe embed for any public song or project. Simply use:

html
Copy
Edit
<iframe
  width="560"
  height="202"
  src="https://www.bandlab.com/embed/?id=fd116604-738c-ed11-9d7a-000d3a3eed2a"
  frameborder="0"
  allowfullscreen>
</iframe>
– Swap out the id=… with your project’s UUID.
– Users get a fully playable BandLab widget with play/pause, waveform, and volume controls—again, right on your page.

3. Elfsight Audio Player Widget
If you just need a slick, configurable audio player (for your Boomy or Uberduck-generated MP3s), Elfsight offers a free plan that lets you:

Build a playlist or single-track player in their visual editor

Copy the generated HTML <div>+<script> snippet

Paste it anywhere on your site

This gives you loop/shuffle, autoplay, custom skins—and you host the audio yourself. 
Elfsight

4. DIY with Uberduck & Boomy APIs
Neither Uberduck.ai nor Boomy offer out-of-the-box widgets yet, but both have REST APIs:

Uberduck.ai – generate a singing TTS WAV/MP3 on the fly; host it in your CDN and embed via a standard HTML5 player.

Boomy – download your 30 s clip on the free tier, store it on your server or S3, then use the same Elfsight widget or a simple <audio> tag:

html
Copy
Edit
<audio controls>
  <source src="/uploads/my-baby-song.mp3" type="audio/mp3">
</audio>
Summary
Interactive embeds: Chrome Music Lab Song Maker and BandLab player

Hosted audio widget: Elfsight Audio Player 
Elfsight

Custom flows: call Uberduck or Boomy, host the resulting MP3, embed with <audio> or the Elfsight widget

With these, kids and teachers never have to leave your site—they can write a line, “Soosify” it, generate the song, and sing it all in one place!
