Okay, you're looking for impactful videos that can serve as engaging training material for preschool teachers, similar to the effect of Rita Pierson's TED Talk or Conscious Discipline's practical examples. Here's a list of video resources and specific talks that often resonate with educators and offer valuable insights:

Inspiring TED Talks & Similar Presentations:

Rita Pierson: "Every Kid Needs a Champion"
Link: You've mentioned this, and it's a classic for a reason! (Easily found on TED.com or YouTube)
Why it's impactful: Pierson's passionate talk emphasizes the importance of human connection, belief in students, and the teacher's role as a champion for every child. It's incredibly motivating.
Sir Ken Robinson: "Do Schools Kill Creativity?"
Link: One of the most-watched TED Talks of all time. (Available on TED.com or YouTube)
Why it's impactful: While not solely focused on preschool, it profoundly challenges conventional education and champions the importance of fostering creativity from a young age. It can inspire preschool teachers to protect and nurture the inherent creativity in young children.
Molly Wright: "How Every Child Can Thrive by Five"
Link: (Search on TED.com or YouTube) This is a powerful talk, often featuring a young child on stage, demonstrating "serve and return" interactions.
Why it's impactful: It vividly illustrates the critical importance of early brain development, positive adult-child interactions (like serve and return), and play in the first five years. It offers concrete examples of what these interactions look like.
Dr. Becky Bailey (Conscious Discipline Founder) - TEDx Talk: "Wiring the Brain for Success"
Link: (Search on YouTube for "Becky Bailey TEDx")
Why it's impactful: Dr. Bailey explains the brain science behind self-regulation and connection in a way that underpins the Conscious Discipline methodology. It provides the "why" for many effective classroom strategies.
Christopher Emdin: "Teach Teachers How to Create Magic"
Link: (Available on TED.com or YouTube)
Why it's impactful: Emdin talks about engaging students by understanding their culture and bringing authentic energy to the classroom. While his examples are often with older students, the principles of authentic engagement and creating an electrifying learning environment are very relevant for preschool.
Angela Lee Duckworth: "Grit: The Power of Passion and Perseverance"
Link: (Available on TED.com or YouTube)
Why it's impactful: This talk explores the importance of "grit" as a key predictor of success. For preschool teachers, it can spark thinking about how to foster perseverance, resilience, and a growth mindset in young learners.

Edutopia:
Link: (Search "Edutopia" on YouTube or visit Edutopia.org and look for their video section)
Why it's impactful: Edutopia produces high-quality videos showcasing evidence-based practices in K-12, but many concepts (especially for early elementary) are adaptable or directly relevant to preschool. They often feature real classrooms and teachers demonstrating techniques related to social-emotional learning, project-based learning, and classroom management.
ZERO TO THREE:
Link: (Check their website zerotothree.org for webinars and video resources, or search their name on YouTube)
Why it's impactful: They have videos focusing on infant and toddler development and interaction, which is foundational knowledge for preschool teachers as well. Look for videos on serve-and-return, supporting emotional regulation in very young children, etc.
Tips for Using These Videos in Training:

Select short clips: For training, you don't always need to show an entire hour-long lecture. Pick the most salient 5-15 minute segments.
Facilitate discussion: After showing a video, have teachers discuss what they observed, what resonated, and how they might apply the concepts in their own classrooms.
Focus on specific strategies: Choose videos that clearly demonstrate a technique you want to highlight (e.g., how to set up a "safe place," how to respond to a tantrum with empathy, how to facilitate child-led inquiry).
Mix inspiration with practical application: Balance motivational talks with videos that show concrete "how-to" examples.
