Here’s a concise list of changes to turn your “easy dodge” prototype into a genuine Frogger-style challenge—more lanes, faster hazards, and just enough complexity to keep it fun and fair:

Switch to Horizontal Lane Spawns

Remove the diagonal drop logic entirely.

Define an array of fixed Y-positions (e.g. laneYs = [200, 300, 400, 500, 600]).

Spawn each obstacle at one of those Y’s, at either X=–width or X=screenWidth+width depending on its direction.

This creates a constant stream of hazards to dodge, not just a one-time drop.

Increase Obstacle Density & Speed by Level

Level 1: 2–3 obstacles per lane at speed 5.

Level 2: 4–5 obstacles per lane at speed 7.

Level 3: 6–7 obstacles per lane at speed 9 (and add special hazard like “GlitterPuddle” that slows you).

Limit Player Movement to Lanes & Columns

Snap the teacher to the nearest lane when moving up/down.

Use a fixed step (e.g. 80 px sideways) so you’re always aligned to dodge horizontally moving obstacles.

Add a “Safe Zone” & Checkpoint

After passing the third lane, move the teacher to a brief checkpoint—reset obstacle positions but preserve your score so you don’t restart from the bottom every collision.

Dynamic Obstacle Timing

Don’t spawn all obstacles at once. Use a per-lane timer so each lane gets a new obstacle every 1.5–3 s (randomized).

Child “Chase” Mechanic

Instead of a stationary baby at the top, let the child drift sideways at a slow, constant speed on the top lane—so you actually have to predict and intercept rather than just step straight up.

Introduce a Second Challenge Mode

Fire Drill: Every 20 s all obstacle speeds boost 25% for 5 s, simulating a drill.

Slow-Mo “Time-Out” Zone: One random lane occasionally slows obstacles by 50%—a tactical breather.

Tweak Collision Consequences

On collision, instead of instantly going to the quiz, play a 0.5 s “hit” animation, subtract a life, and respawn at the last checkpoint—so you feel the penalty but keep your momentum.

Polish with Feedback

Screen Shake + SFX on near-misses (when obstacle passes within 20 px).

Combo Meter: Dodge 5 hazards in a row → “Flow Mode” for 3 s (points x2).
