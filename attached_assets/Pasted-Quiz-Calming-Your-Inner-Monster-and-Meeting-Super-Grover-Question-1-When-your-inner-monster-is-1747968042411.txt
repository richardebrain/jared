Quiz: Calming Your Inner Monster (and Meeting Super Grover!)

Question 1:
When your inner monster is having a meltdown and wants to throw things and shout, what's the video's surprisingly simple (and less destructive) advice?
A. Give it a tiny gold trophy for 'Most Dramatic Hissy Fit'.
B. Teach it to yodel its frustrations away from a mountaintop.
C. Do some 'belly breathes' and chill your inner monster out.
D. Offer it a complex Sudoku puzzle to distract its tiny monster brain.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: It's a calming action involving your tummy and not requiring any cleanup!

Question 2:
If your 'mad monster' pops up, what fabulous facial expression does the song say it will inspire you to make?
A. A look of utter bewilderment, like it just saw a pineapple tap-dancing.
B. A 'mad monster face'.
C. The 'Blue Steel' look, hoping to mesmerize the monster into submission.
D. A very convincing impression of a sleepy sloth.
Correct Answer: B
Hint: It's a face that perfectly matches your monster's grumpy mood!

Question 3:
According to the video, when your inner monster is feeling mad, angry, or bad, what two chaotic activities does it want to engage in?
A. It wants to take up knitting to make tiny monster sweaters.
B. It wants to throw things and shout.
C. It wants to write a strongly worded letter to the editor about the lack of quality monster snacks.
D. It wants to have a serious, sit-down discussion about its feelings over a cup of herbal tea.
Correct Answer: B
Hint: Think destructive and loud!

Question 4:
When you successfully 'come that monster' (calm it down), what lovely thing does the video say you'll bring out?
A. More monster snacks (preferably crunchy).
B. A tiny crown and scepter.
C. Monster love.
D. The ability to file its taxes correctly.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: It's a warm, fuzzy feeling associated with monsters, apparently!

Question 5:
After you've done your belly breathes and calmed your inner monster, what fantastic feeling does the song say you'll experience?
A. A sudden craving for pickles and ice cream.
B. You feel like yourself again.
C. An irresistible urge to do the cha-cha.
D. You briefly gain the ability to talk to squirrels.
Correct Answer: B
Hint: It's about returning to your normal, wonderful state of being!

Question 6:
When Super Grover makes his grand appearance, what are some of the amazing things he proclaims about himself?
A. He arrives on a unicycle juggling flaming torches.
B. He's 'with the GG,' defying gravity, graceful as a swan, and has gargantuan muscles.
C. He's looking for his lost car keys and seems a bit stressed.
D. He's selling cookies for the local Grover Scout troop.
Correct Answer: B
Hint: He mentions his associates, his floaty abilities, his elegance, and his impressive build!

Question 7:
Super Grover mentions he's 'with the GG.' What does 'GG' stand for?
A. The 'GG Spot,' a notoriously hard-to-find location.
B. His 'Good Groceries,' because he promotes healthy eating.
C. It's not specified, remaining one of life's great mysteries, like why socks disappear in the laundry.
D. His 'Granny's Go-kart,' his preferred mode of transport.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: The video doesn't explicitly state what 'GG' stands for, making it a bit of a Super Grover enigma!

Question 8:
To what elegant creature does Super Grover compare his gracefulness?
A. A fluffy cloud.
B. A slightly tipsy flamingo.
C. A swan.
D. A runaway shopping cart on a downhill slope.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: Think of a majestic white bird often found in lakes.

Question 9:
What specific type of breathing does the video suggest is 'how you do it' to calm your inner monster?
A. Throwing a tantrum.
B. Making a mad monster face.
C. Belly breathing.
D. Shouting affirmations very, very loudly.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: It's a kind of breath that involves a particular part of your torso!

Question 10:
What is the very first piece of advice given in the video for when the 'monster inside you is mad'?
A. Give it a cookie and a warm glass of milk.
B. Breathe.
C. Watch Super Grover videos until it forgets why it's mad.
D. Engage in vigorous interpretive dance.
Correct Answer: B
Hint: It's a one-word instruction, repeated often!

Question 11:
According to the lyrics, when your 'mad monster' makes an appearance, what two things does 'he make you wanna' do?
A. Push and shop (or shout).
B. Bake a cake and write poetry.
C. Do belly breathing and feel like yourself again.
D. Defy gravity and announce your muscles are gargantuan.
Correct Answer: A
Hint: One is a physical action against something/someone, and the other is a loud vocal action (the transcript says 'shop' but it's likely 'shout').

Question 12:
How does Super Grover describe the size of his muscles?
A. A very small, slightly dusty ant.
B. Non-existent, as he relies on pure grace.
C. Gargantuan.
D. Made entirely of fluffy marshmallows.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: It's a rather large and impressive-sounding word!
