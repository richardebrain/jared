Mindful Morning Online Course (≈ 60 min total)
▶️ Module 0: Welcome & Why It Matters (5 min)
Objective: Ignite passion for the power of ages 0–5.

Watch (2 min): Brain-development time-lapse + “90% by age 5” stat video.

Reflect (2 min): In your journal, write one word that describes your own early-years narrative.

Quick Quiz (1 min):

T/F: “By age 5, the majority of our neural connections are formed.” (Answer: True)

▶️ Module 1: Grounding with Breath (15 min)
Objective: Learn a simple daily breath routine to regulate emotion.

Watch (4 min): 3-minute Joe Dispenza “Morning Coherence” breathwork video.

Practice (5 min):

3 rounds of 4-7-8 breathing:

Inhale 4 sec

Hold 7 sec

Exhale 8 sec

Use a simple on-screen timer or app.

Journal Prompt (3 min):

“How did your heart, mind and body feel before vs. after?”

Knowledge Check (3 min):

MCQ: Which brain region benefits most from paced breathing?

Prefrontal cortex ✔

Occipital lobe

Cerebellum

Brainstem

▶️ Module 2: Building Your Narrative with Affirmations (20 min)
Objective: Harness “I am …” statements to rewire self-image.

Read & Watch (5 min):

Short article/video on neuroplasticity of affirmations.

Create Your 3 Affirmations (5 min):

Choose three “I am …” statements (e.g. “I am calm,” “I am creative,” “I am a world-changer”).

Record yourself saying them (audio note or video clip).

Worksheet (5 min):

For each affirmation, jot a specific classroom scenario where you’ll use it (e.g. before circle time).

Quiz (5 min):

Match the fact to the affirmation benefit:

A) strengthens positive neural circuits

B) reduces cortisol spikes

C) increases creative problem-solving

▶️ Module 3: Cultivating Daily Gratitude (15 min)
Objective: Build an evidence-based gratitude habit that lowers stress.

Watch (2 min): Short clip summarizing studies on gratitude and well-being.

Journal (5 min):

Write three things you’re grateful for today (big or small).

Practice (3 min):

Draft a quick “thank-you” note or text for someone (colleague, friend, family).

Science Check (5 min):

MCQ / True-False on cortisol reduction and improved sleep quality from gratitude practice.

▶️ Module 4: Your Mindful Morning Plan & Badge (5 min)
Objective: Commit to bringing these practices into your classroom.

Plan Sheet:

Choose one breathing exercise, one affirmation slot, and one gratitude ritual you’ll use tomorrow morning.

Digital Badge:

Upon completion of the four knowledge checks (quiz questions), download your “Mindful Morning Practitioner” badge and share it on your staff-portal profile.

Reflection:

In 1–2 sentences, answer: “Why does leading a mindful morning matter for our little learners—and for our world?”

Tips for Maximum Engagement
Embedded Checks: Use your LMS’s quiz feature after each module so completion unlocks the next.

Multimedia: Host videos on YouTube or Vimeo and embed them; keep each under 5 minutes.

Journaling: Provide a downloadable PDF “Mindful Morning Journal” or simply use a digital text field.

Interactive Worksheets: Include fillable PDFs or Google Forms for the Affirmation & Plan sheets.

Badge Issuance: Automate via LMS badge feature or send a shareable image upon passing all quizzes.
