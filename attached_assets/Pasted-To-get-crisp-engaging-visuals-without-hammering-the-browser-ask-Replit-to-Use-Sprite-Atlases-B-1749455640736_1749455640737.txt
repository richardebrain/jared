To get crisp, engaging visuals without hammering the browser, ask Replit to:

Use Sprite Atlases & Batched Blits

Pack all obstacle, avatar, and UI images into one or two PNG atlases so Pygame can draw them in a single texture bind.

Blit entire sprite sheets then use subsurface() rather than loading individual files.

Pre‐Render Static Layers

Draw your background (sidewalk, lane lines, playground fencing) once to an off‐screen Surface, then blit that each frame—only your moving objects get re‐drawn every tick.

Convert Surfaces for Speed

After loading or creating any image Surface, immediately call .convert() or .convert_alpha() so Pygame stores it in the native video format and speeds up blitting.

Limit Resolution & Scale Wisely

Keep your canvas at a modest 600×800 (or even 480×640 on mobile). If you need larger art, use integer scales (×1, ×2) rather than smooth resizes every frame.

Optimize Your Particle System

Cap active particles (e.g. 30 at once), reuse dead particles from a pool instead of creating new objects, and pre‐draw your “confetti” shapes to tiny sprite frames rather than computing shapes on the fly.

Use Simple Geometry & Vector Shapes

For UI elements (buttons, health bars, combo meters), draw rectangles, circles, and polygons with Pygame’s primitives—no need for heavyweight images.

Parallax with Minimal Layers

If you add a moving sky or distant playground props, limit yourself to two parallax layers (slow + medium scroll) and move them by small offsets—keeps the scene dynamic but light.

Palette & Style Consistency

Pick a tight, 8–16 color palette and reuse those colors for obstacles, avatars, and UI. Fewer unique pixels = faster texture uploads.

Frame‐Rate Independent Animations

Animate via dt‐based frame switches (e.g. advance animation frame every 0.1 s) so you never drop frames or overload at higher speeds.

Profile & Throttle

Add a simple FPS counter in debug mode. If you dip below ~50 FPS, automatically reduce particle counts or obstacle density until you bounce back above 55 FPS.
