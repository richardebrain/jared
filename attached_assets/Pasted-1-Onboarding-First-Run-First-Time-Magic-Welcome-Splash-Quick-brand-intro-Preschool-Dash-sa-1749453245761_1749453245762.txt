1. Onboarding & First Run (“First-Time Magic”)
Welcome Splash

Quick brand intro: “Preschool Dash—save the kiddos!”

Show a Daily Spin Wheel (“Try it free!”) with guaranteed small reward on first play (e.g. +50 coins or a “Compassion Shield” power-up).

Turbo Tutorial

2–3 swipes through core mechanics (lane-swipe, jump, slide).

Tutorial gives you one full Power-Up crate at the end so managers feel rewarded before the real chase begins.

2. Core Run Loop (“Run → Crash → Question → Reward”)
Run Phase

Endless-runner chasing the child through three lanes of Preschool Parkway.

Randomly spawn Loot Crates at hard-to-reach spots—crate icons say “Quiz Booster” or “Sticker Storm.”

Crash & Quiz Phase

On collision: pause → safety question → either +10 points/respawn or level reset.

Immediately after correct answer, drop a Mini-Loot Spinner (3-segment chance: “+5 Coins,” “Extra Life,” “Speed Dash”).

Reward Tally

Show score + coins + spins + badges.

Offer a “Double-Or-Nothing” prompt: gamble today’s coins on a 50/50 spinner for double or lose half—encourages risk/reward play.

3. Session End & Meta-Progression
Level Completion Screen

Big “Level Up!” banner, breakdown of coins, crates, spins earned.

A “Mystery Crate” appears: click to open (randomly grants one of: new costume, rare power-up, extra quiz skip token).

Daily Login Bonus

Each new day grants a free spin on a 5-segment “Rainbow Wheel” (varied odds: small coin stash, big coin jackpot, rare “Super Teacher Cape,” extra life, or “Better Quiz Skip”).

Streak Tracker & Stash

Track consecutive days played—5-day streak unlocks a guaranteed Rare Loot Crate.

Show “Streak Bar” on home screen to encourage return visits.

4. Long-Term Engagement
Gacha-Style “Power-Up Draw”

Offer a shop where coins buy “Power-Up Cards” (randomized pulls with displayed rarity odds).

Cards include one-time shields, multi-ball modes, or special “Scenario-Save” tokens that auto-answer one quiz question correctly.

Seasonal Events & Limited-Time Offers

Holiday themes (e.g. “Pumpkin-Patch Panic” level) with exclusive crates only available during the event window.

Countdown timers (“2 days left to claim the Glitter-Glue Gauntlet skin!”) drive FOMO.

Social Hooks

Leaderboards: Top scores per week earn virtual badges (“Teacher Titan,” “Quiz Conqueror”).

Share-Your-Run: One-click social share of your best run clip or “Mystery Crate haul.”

Achievement Badges

Long-tail goals: “Answer 100 Quiz Questions,” “Open 50 Crates,” “Run 10 km total.”

Badge collection displayed in a “Trophy Room” encourages completionism.

5. Ideal Play Flow
Entry Point:

Daily Spin → Turbo Tutorial (only first time) → Core Run

During Run:

Collect coins, loot crates, power-ups → dodge obstacles → occasional quiz resets

Post-Run Rewards:

Level/score screen → open loot crates → optional gamble (“Double-Or-Nothing”) → claim daily streak bonus

Meta Engagement:

Visit Shop for gacha draws → check Achievements → peek Leaderboards → schedule next play via push reminder
