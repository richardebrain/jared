1. Dynamic Power-Ups & Boosts
“Time-Out Timer”
Catch this floating hourglass and slow all obstacles by 50% for 5 seconds—buy yourself a breather (and reinforce the idea of a brief, calm “time-out” when things get overwhelming).

“Sticker Storm”
Snag a whirlwind of virtual reward stickers (stars, smiley faces, high-five icons). Each sticker dropped on a safety-question hit nets bonus points and becomes a “cheat-sheet” hint if you miss a question later.

“Team Rally”
Collect three “Teacher Teammate” icons (mini avatars of Conner, Tanner, OJ and Jeremiah) to trigger a brief “co-op mode” where obstacles part like a crowd, modeling how asking for help clears chaos.

2. Sillier Obstacles & Random Events
“Glitter Glue Puddles”
Step in one and you’re stuck for 1 s—but tapping the “Wipe Up” key combo frees you. Teaches quick clean-up habits!

“Fire Drill Frenzy”
At random intervals, an alarm bell rings and all obstacles speed up 20% for 5 s—simulating an evacuation drill and testing your calm-under-pressure reflexes.

“Surprise Snack Cart”
Occasionally rolls by and drops “Goldfish Bombs”: if you hit one you get extra points—but you must instantly answer a question about allergen protocols to claim them.

3. Mini-Games Between Levels
After each level, unlock a 15 s “Micro-Module”:

Tap-Quiz Dash: Rapid-fire 5 true/false questions on that level’s domain.

Drag-and-Drop Chart: Reassemble a “Visual Schedule” board in 10 s to unlock a speed boost for the next level.

Match-the-Milestone: Pair a toddler milestone (pincer grasp, first words) with the right age range—correct matches give you a “Milestone Medal.”

4. Customization & Progression
Avatar Unlocks
Earn costumes (“Super-Teacher Cape,” “Pajama-Day Bear Suit,” “Ninja-Quiet Shoes”) that come with tiny gameplay perks (slightly faster sprint, quieter movement to sneak past some obstacles).

Classroom Hub
Between runs, visit your “Classroom” screen to place items you’ve unlocked—posters, rugs, even a live digital fish tank. Each décor unlock boosts passive score multipliers (e.g. +5 % for having a “Handwashing” poster).

5. Social & Challenge Modes
Daily “Headcount Challenge”
Run a quick, 30-s mini-level where you must dodge as many “Missing Child” shadows as possible. High scores get featured on a weekly leaderboard.

Co-op Rescue Run
Two-player mode (split-screen or online) where one teacher chases the child and the other drives a “Safety Cart,” laying down power-up puddles. Teaches teamwork!

Speed-Run Sprint
How fast can you finish all three levels without ever hitting an obstacle? Unlocks a “Master of Calm” badge.

6. Narrative & Easter Eggs
“Storybook Snippets”
Occasionally you’ll spot a moving book icon—run over it to pause and see a 5 s animated vignette of a preschool scenario (nap-time rescue, playground heroics).

Hidden Easter Eggs
Drop into a secret “Finger-Paint Fury” bonus lane if you collect three “Paint-Can” power-ups in one run—complete it to earn a rainbow paddle skin.
