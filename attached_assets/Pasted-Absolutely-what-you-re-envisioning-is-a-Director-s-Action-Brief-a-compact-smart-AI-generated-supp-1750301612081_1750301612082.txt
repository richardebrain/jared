🧩 MentorMe Director’s Action Brief
🔍 Issue Focus: Employee Tardiness
🧠 1. Suspected Root Causes (To Explore Thoughtfully)
“Behavior is a signal. Understand it before correcting it.” – Brené Brown

Personal Life Stress: Family, transportation, health issues

Burnout or disengagement: Dread coming in, lack of energy or connection

Unclear expectations or lack of accountability culture

Poor sleep hygiene or time management skills

Lack of ownership or buy-in around their role

📝 Prompt to use with Director:

“What have you noticed about their general mood or energy level? Is this new behavior or ongoing? Any patterns—specific days, people, or roles?”

📋 2. Director’s First Action Steps
“Approach first with curiosity, not confrontation.”

✅ Schedule a private 1:1 conversation within 48 hours.

✅ Use open-ended questions:

“How are things going for you outside of work?”

“I’ve noticed you’ve had some trouble getting in on time. Can you tell me about that?”

✅ Reflect and summarize what you hear—without judgment.

✅ Determine if support is needed: childcare, time shift, accountability partner

✅ Clarify expectations clearly and kindly

🗣️ 3. How to Lead the Conversation (Tone & Phrasing)
Open with empathy:

“You matter to this team, and I want to understand what’s going on so we can move forward together.”

Hold accountability:

“Being on time is a basic standard here—and I believe you can meet it with the right plan.”

Plan together:

“What would help you arrive on time consistently? What do you think is a reasonable next step?”

🚦 4. Suggested Motivational Insights from Experts
Expert	Tool/Quote	Application
Brené Brown	“Clear is kind.”	Be direct, avoid vague hints. Say what needs to be said with empathy.
Tony Robbins	"Change happens when the pain of staying the same is greater than the pain of change."	Help them link tardiness to lost trust, opportunity, or team morale—but from a place of belief in their potential.
Simon Sinek	“Start with why.”	Reconnect them to the mission. Why are they here? Why does this job matter to them?
Mel Robbins	The 5-Second Rule	Teach them to use mental triggers to stop hesitation and act faster in the morning routine.

🎯 5. Next Steps & Accountability Plan
Goal	Plan	Follow-up
Arrive on time 5 days this week	Set alarm 30 min earlier, no snooze. Accountability text from peer or you	Daily check-in via Vojo or morning text
Reflect on “why I work here”	Write 3 reasons, share privately with you or Vojo	Review during next 1:1
Agree on new consequence if continued	First step: written reminder	Review in 2 weeks, escalate only if needed

📈 6. What’s Worked for Other Centers:
✅ Offering a temporary shift swap to rebuild confidence

✅ Giving employee a small leadership task to reignite buy-in

✅ Creating a “Tardy Jar” challenge: if on time all week, earn a reward or recognition

✅ Using Vojo check-in prompts to log how they’re feeling each morning before arrival

❤️ Final Word to the Director:
"Be firm. Be fair. Be curious. Behavior that’s corrected with compassion can unlock loyalty, growth, and maturity. Don’t just correct them—coach them."
