Quiz: Rita Pierson: Every Kid Needs a Champion - Key Ideas

Question 1:
According to Rita Pierson, what is one of the things rarely discussed in education reform that is crucial for learning?
A. The frequency of standardized testing.
B. The value and importance of human connection and relationships.
C. The amount of homework assigned.
D. The newest educational technology.
Correct Answer: B
Hint: It's about how people connect with each other in the learning environment.

Question 2:
How did Rita Pierson say she handled a student who missed 18 out of 20 questions on a quiz?
A. She told the student they needed to study much harder and retake the test.
B. She gave the student an F and a stern lecture.
C. She put a '+2' on the paper with a big smiley face, focusing on the questions the student got right.
D. She suggested the student might not be capable of the work.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: Her approach was designed to encourage the student and build self-esteem, rather than highlight the failure.

Question 3:
What was Rita Pierson's response to her colleague who said, 'They don't pay me to like the kids'?
A. Kids learn best when they are afraid of their teacher.
B. You should seek first to be understood, then to understand.
C. Kids don't learn from people they don't like.
D. Liking students is not part of a teacher's job description.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: It was a concise statement about the prerequisite for students to learn from a teacher.

Question 4:
Rita Pierson quotes James Comer, stating, 'No significant learning can occur without...'
A. ...a significant amount of funding.
B. ...a perfectly quiet classroom.
C. ...a lot of rigorous testing.
D. ...a significant relationship.
Correct Answer: D
Hint: This concept is about the bond between individuals, not resources or methods.

Question 5:
What 'bright idea' did Rita Pierson share for raising the self-esteem of a class that was academically deficient?
A. She told them it was because they were the most academically advanced.
B. She said it was a random assignment by the school computer.

D. She explained it was to balance out the other classes with more challenging students.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: It involved a special reason she gave them for being in her class.

Question 6:
What was part of the saying Rita Pierson taught her students to affirm their self-worth?

Question 7:

Question 9:
When discussing not liking every student, what does Rita Pierson say teachers must do so students never know it?

B. Become great actors and actresses.

Question 10:
What does Rita Pierson mean when she says, 'Every child deserves a champion'?
A. A student who is always afraid to take risks.
B. An adult who will never give up on them, understands connection, and insists they become their best.
C. A system that relies solely on standardized test scores to measure worth.
D. A guarantee that they will never fail or face hardship.
Correct Answer: B
Hint: A champion is a specific type of adult who provides unwavering support and belief.

Question 11:
What is Rita Pierson's stated belief about the emotional experience of teaching and learning?

B. Teachers should only teach when they feel like it and when policies make perfect sense.
C. Teaching and learning should bring joy.
D. The main goal of education is to ensure all students get high-paying jobs.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: She connects the educational process with a positive emotion.

Question 12:
