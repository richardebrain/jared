Quiz: Cosmic Kids Zen Den: Listening Skills & Spidey Senses

Question 1:

B. Freshly baked bread and a hint of existential dread.

D. Wet dog and the faint smell of regret.

Hint: One is a tropical fruit, and the other is a popular Italian dish!

Question 2:

C. A 'meh' 5, indicating you only listen when a cookie is involved.

Hint: It's the top score, suggesting superhero-level listening!

Question 3:

Question 4:
