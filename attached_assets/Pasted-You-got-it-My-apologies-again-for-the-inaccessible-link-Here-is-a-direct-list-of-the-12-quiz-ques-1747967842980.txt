Here is a direct list of the 12 quiz questions based on the "Cosmic Kids - Coco the Butterfly" yoga adventure:

Quiz: Cosmic Kids: Coco the Butterfly Yoga Adventure

Question 1:
At the beginning of the story, how does Coco the butterfly feel about getting her wings?
A. Excited and eager to use them immediately.
B. She is sad, doesn't want her wings, and wishes to remain a caterpillar in her cocoon.
C. She is very proud and shows them off to all the other butterflies.
D. She is confused and doesn't understand what wings are for.
Correct Answer: B
Hint: Think about Coco's reaction to becoming a butterfly and leaving her cocoon.

Question 2:
How do Jamie and the viewers find out they are going to meet Coco the butterfly?
A. By looking through a telescope.
B. Coco sends them an invitation.
C. By looking through their 'cosminoculars'.
D. A friendly bird tells them about Coco.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: It involves a special way of looking that Jamie introduces.

Question 3:
What realization does Coco start to have while sheltering in the dark, gloomy cave during the thunderstorm?
A. She thinks her wings might not be so bad after all.
B. She decides she definitely wants to be a caterpillar forever.
C. She makes friends with a bat.
D. She finds a map to a sunnier meadow.
Correct Answer: A
Hint: The experience in the cave helps Coco appreciate something she initially disliked.

Question 4:
After the rain has passed and a rainbow appears, who comes out and meets Coco?
A. A ladybug who loves to paint.
B. Suzy the snail, who has an old shell.
C. A caterpillar who is also afraid of change.
D. A wise old owl who gives Coco advice.
Correct Answer: B
Hint: This character moves slowly and carries their home on their back.

Question 5:
What does Suzy the snail tell Coco she sometimes wishes for?
A. She wishes she had colorful wings like Coco.
B. She wishes her shell was lighter so she could move faster.
C. She wishes she could spread some wings and fly away like Coco.
D. She wishes Coco would help her decorate her shell.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: Suzy admires something about Coco that is very different from her own way of life.

Question 6:
How do Coco's wings finally begin to stretch, uncurl, and unfold?
A. When she takes a deep breath and decides to be brave.
B. As she reaches forwards to get her cup of tea, letting go of her cocoon.
C. After Suzy the snail gives her an encouraging speech.
D. When the sun comes out fully after the rainbow.
Correct Answer: B
Hint: It happens during the tea party inside Suzy the snail's shell.

Question 7:
When Jamie first sees Coco the butterfly through the cosminoculars, what yoga pose is Coco doing?
A. Rainbow pose
B. Cocoon pose
C. Butterfly pose
D. Snail pose
Correct Answer: A
Hint: It's a pose associated with a colorful arc in the sky.

Question 8:
What important message about change is shared with Coco when she is initially sad about her wings?
A. That change is always scary and should be avoided.
B. That wings are not as good as having a shell.
C. That sometimes change can be a good thing.
D. That caterpillars are happier than butterflies.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: It's a positive outlook on new experiences.

Question 9:
Which set of yoga poses represents the cozy armchair, the table, and the bowl of leaves found inside Suzy the snail's shell?
A. Chair pose, Table pose, and Bowl pose.
B. Bicycle pose, Thunder pose, and Lightning pose.
C. Door pose, Snail pose, and Cocoon pose.
D. Rainbow pose, Butterfly pose, and Hello Sun pose.
Correct Answer: A
Hint: Think about the furniture and items seen during the tea party.

Question 10:
In the story, how do Jamie and the viewers represent the feeling of rain?
A. Pitter-pattering their fingers on themselves.
B. Spinning around with arms wide.
C. Clapping their hands to the sky.
D. Sweeping an arm over their ear in an arc.
Correct Answer: A
Hint: It's a gentle tapping movement.

Question 11:
Which yoga pose do Jamie and the viewers do to 'cycle to the meadow' to find Coco?
A. Bicycle pose
B. Butterfly pose
C. Snail pose
D. Chair pose
Correct Answer: A
Hint: It involves lying on your back and pedaling your feet.

Question 12:
What is a key lesson Coco the butterfly learns by the end of the yoga adventure?
A. To always stay in your comfort zone and avoid new things.
B. That being a snail is better than being a butterfly.
C. That sometimes change can be good, and appreciating your own unique qualities is important.
D. That tea parties can solve all problems.
Correct Answer: C
Hint: Think about Coco's feelings at the start versus how she might feel after her wings unfold and she experiences new things.
